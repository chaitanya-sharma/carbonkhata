Get your 2026 shipments signed before the first EU deadline

Who should apply

  • Exporters of fasteners and steel articles (HS 7318, 7326) to the EU, in Ludhiana or nearby
  • Stockists in Mandi Gobindgarh and Ludhiana that sell to them
  • Mills with verified CBAM reports for wire rod, bars or coil

Timeline

  • December 2026Agreements with EEPC and associations; verifier slots booked; first mill live
