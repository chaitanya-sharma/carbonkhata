Solutions

Each party does one thing, and the exporter's file is complete

Carbon Khata works only when the mill, the stockist, the exporter and the verifier each add their part. Here is what each one does, what it costs them and what they get.

Exporters

File on actual emissions and keep the saving

A small bolt maker in Ludhiana pays CBAM on India's default value because the mill's report never reaches it. Carbon Khata brings the report with each steel lot and turns the exporter's own bills into a monitoring file a verifier can sign.

Stockists

Split a lot without losing its report

Most Ludhiana workshops buy from stockists in Mandi Gobindgarh, not from the mill. When a stockist bills a sale, the Tally add-on attaches the lot and its mill report, and the ledger blocks any sale beyond the tonnes bought.

Steel mills

Publish once, reach every downstream buyer

Large Indian mills already prepare verified CBAM reports for their own EU customers. Carbon Khata carries that report to the hundreds of small buyers the mill reaches through stockists, at no cost to the mill.

Accredited verifiers

Ten sites a week, evidence ready on arrival

Every site needs a physical visit in the first year. Only five bodies hold EU CBAM verifier accreditation, so capacity is scarce. Carbon Khata books cluster campaigns, prepares standard monitoring plans and evidence packs, and leaves verification and signing to the accredited body.

EU importers

Fewer certificates, and origin evidence for the quota

Authorised declarants buy certificates for every tonne they import. Actual-data files from Indian suppliers cut that number, and the mill's heat numbers show where the steel was first melted and poured.

Steel quota

Since 1 July 2026, steel above a country's quota pays a 50% duty and needs melt-and-pour evidence (Regulation (EU) 2026/1384).

Lenders

Lend for cleaner steel against signed records

Once an exporter has a year of verified emissions, a lender can finance its switch to lower-carbon steel without its own emissions check. The same records serve the UK CBAM from January 2027 and India's carbon market.
