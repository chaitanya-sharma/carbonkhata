Trust and compliance

Built on the rules as written, with a verifier's signature on every file

Carbon Khata prepares data. Accredited verifiers check and sign it. This page lists the rules we follow, what we do with data and AI, and who is responsible for each control.

Reg. (EU) 2023/956CBAM core mandate & declaration schedule

What it says: CBAM applies to iron and steel; certificates payable on 2026 imports, first declaration by 30 Sep 2027.

Carbon Khata function: Builds the actual-data declaration package with full audit trail that the EU importer submits into the CBAM Registry.

IR 2025/2547 Art. 14Multi-installation precursor mass-weighting

What it says: Precursors from several installations are weighted by quantity; an unverified precursor takes its default.

Carbon Khata function: Blends mill certificates weighted by tonnage. If 70% of rod is traced to 2.35 tCO₂/t and 30% is untraced, only the 30% takes India's default of 4.73.

IR 2025/2620Free-allocation benchmark deduction

What it says: Free-allocation adjustment = EU benchmark × CBAM factor (97.5% in 2026, 95% in 2027, 90% in 2028).

Carbon Khata function: Carries free-allocation data with the mill's emissions figure to accurately calculate net certificate obligations per tonne.

IR 2025/2621, 2026/1740Country defaults & statutory mark-ups

What it says: Country default values with an escalating mark-up of +10% in 2026, +20% in 2027, and +30% from 2028.

Carbon Khata function: Renders live side-by-side cost comparisons demonstrating the punitive cost of defaulting versus submitting verified data.

IR 2025/2546Accredited site audit standards

What it says: Mandatory physical site visit in the first year; virtual visits or waivers permitted later under strict conditions.

Carbon Khata function: Organizes multi-plant cluster batching campaigns so international accredited verifiers visit 10–12 Ludhiana workshops in a single week, slashing individual audit costs by 66%.

Reg. (EU) 2026/138450% steel quota duty & melt-and-pour proof

What it says: Steel quota with a 50% out-of-quota duty and mandatory melt-and-pour country of origin evidence from 1 July 2026.

Carbon Khata function: Links every fastener batch to primary mill heat numbers, proving melt-and-pour origin in India and avoiding the 50% duty.

CGST Notification 10/2023GST e-invoicing interoperability

What it says: GST e-invoices mandatory above ₹5 crore turnover threshold.

Carbon Khata function: Uses 64-character Invoice Reference Number (IRN) as an immutable cryptographic index key without extracting proprietary commercial pricing.

Data protection

Statutory fields only, hosted in India

We collect what an EU file needs and nothing more. Every party consents to what it shares, and buyers see emissions per lot, never prices.

Consent under the DPDP Act

Consent is recorded for every field a party shares. Owner details in proprietorships are treated as personal data.

What buyers see

Emissions per tonne, route, period and heat numbers. Never buying prices, margins, customer lists or process recipes.

Breach reporting

A named data protection officer reports any breach within 72 hours.

How we use AI

Software reads documents. People approve every field.

What the software does

  • Reads fuel bills, weighbridge slips and mill certificates in Punjabi, Hindi and English
  • Flags gaps between fuel use and output, and between slips and invoices
  • Queues every extracted field for a reviewer

What it never does

  • Fill in missing data or estimate values
  • Adjust numbers to lower a result
  • Certify a file: an EU-accredited verifier signs every one
