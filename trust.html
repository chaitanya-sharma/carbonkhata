Trust and compliance

Built on the rules as written, with a verifier's signature on every file

Carbon Khata prepares data. Accredited verifiers check and sign it. This page lists the rules we follow, what we do with data and AI, and who is responsible for each control.

Data protection

Statutory fields only, hosted in India

We collect what an EU file needs and nothing more. Every party consents to what it shares, and buyers see emissions per lot, never prices.

Consent under the DPDP Act

Consent is recorded for every field a party shares. Owner details in proprietorships are treated as personal data.

What buyers see

Emissions per tonne, route, period and heat numbers. Never buying prices, margins, customer lists or process recipes.

Breach reporting

A named data protection officer reports any breach within 72 hours.

How we use AI

Software reads documents. People approve every field.

What the software does

  • Reads fuel bills, weighbridge slips and mill certificates in Punjabi, Hindi and English
  • Flags gaps between fuel use and output, and between slips and invoices
  • Queues every extracted field for a reviewer

What it never does

  • Fill in missing data or estimate values
  • Adjust numbers to lower a result
  • Certify a file: an EU-accredited verifier signs every one
