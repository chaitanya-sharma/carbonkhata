The Dangerous Myth

"97.5% free allocation in 2026 means almost zero CBAM cost."

Judges and exporters often assume that because the 2026 phase-in factor is 97.5%, importers pay only 2.5% of total carbon emissions, making CBAM negligible until 2030.

Assumed deduction97.5% of 6.29 tCO₂/t
Assumed payable0.16 certificates (~₹1,300/t)
❌ Flawed assumption: Free allocation DOES NOT apply to total emissions
➔
The Statutory Truth

The 97.5% factor applies ONLY to the EU benchmark (1.53 t), NOT to actual emissions.

Under EU CBAM rules, free allocation reduces only the 1.53 t EU benchmark (1.53 × 97.5% = 1.49 t SEFA). A bolt on India's default carries 6.29 t, so 4.8 certificates per tonne (~₹39,000/t) remain payable!

Gross default emissions6.29 tCO₂/t
SEFA deduction (1.53 × 97.5%)−1.49 tCO₂/t
Net payable certificates4.8 certificates / tonne (~₹39,000/t)

The Architecture

The Three Pillars of Carbon Khata

How our decentralized precursor data rail and cluster verification turn an existential MSME export threat into a durable competitive advantage.

01
Pillar 1 · Data Infrastructure

Precursor Data Rail

The primary mill's verified emissions report and heat numbers travel directly with the lot through stockists to the MSME workshop. Our Tally Prime add-on tags GST e-invoices with zero workflow change, while our cryptographic mass-balance cap strictly blocks overselling beyond tonnes purchased.

  • Mill report & heat numbers travel with every lot
  • Tally Prime add-on integrates into existing billing
  • Mass-balance cap strictly blocks overselling
02
Pillar 2 · Cluster Economics

Batched Verification

Independent year-1 site visits are mandated by EU law, but individual audits cost ₹17.5 Lakh. We organize 10 sites per week in the Ludhiana cluster with standardized monitoring plans and shared verifier travel, dropping verification costs to ₹6.0 Lakh per plant (−66% savings).

  • 10 sites / week in Ludhiana fastener cluster
  • Shared travel + templates slash audit cost
  • ₹17.5L → ₹6.0L per site (−66% cost reduction)
03
Pillar 3 · Growth & Finance

Carbon-to-Cash

Verified carbon data doesn't just avoid penalties; it unlocks commercial value. Audited records enable low-interest green loans at SIDBI and commercial banks, prepare exporters for the UK CBAM in January 2027, and power matching with scrap-based lower-carbon steel suppliers.

  • Green loans unlocked at SIDBI and partner banks
  • UK CBAM ready for January 2027 enforcement
  • Lower-carbon steel procurement matching
