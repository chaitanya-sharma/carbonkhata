How the figure is built

Method

Certificates per tonne equal embedded emissions minus the free-allocation adjustment, which is the EU benchmark times the CBAM factor (IR 2025/2620). Steel counts direct emissions only.

Default values

India: bolts 5.72, hot-rolled coil 4.28, wire rod about 4.3 tCO₂/t (IR 2025/2621 as amended by 2026/1740), plus a mark-up of 10% in 2026, 20% in 2027 and 30% from 2028.

What we leave out

No deduction for Indian carbon prices: the EU has not recognised any under Article 9. Default filings use the higher 1.53 t benchmark, so the saving shown is conservative.
