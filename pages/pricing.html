Pricing questions

When do I pay the filing fee?

When the verifier signs your file, so you pay when you sell, months before your EU buyer surrenders certificates in September 2027.

What if the EU certificate price falls?

At €60 a certificate the same exporter still saves ₹72.6 L, a return of 8.5 times all costs. If batching also failed and the verifier cost ₹17.5 L, the return would still be 3.6 times.

Is there a charge during the pilot?

The first file is free in the Ludhiana pilot, and we co-fund the first verification campaign.
