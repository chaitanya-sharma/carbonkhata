The problem

Small exporters lose the mill's data on the way to them

India's large mills already measure and verify their emissions. The report stops at the stockist, so the bolt maker who exports to Europe has nothing to file, and the EU charges its buyer on India's default value.

Reports don't survive a lot split

Stockists in Mandi Gobindgarh split mill coils into small lots for Ludhiana workshops. The mill's emissions report is not passed on with the invoice.

Plant records sit on paper

Fuel bills, weighbridge slips and production logs are paper or WhatsApp photos. Few MSMEs have a monitoring plan or anyone trained in CBAM.

Verification is scarce and costly

Every site needs an accredited verifier's visit in year one. Done alone, a small plant pays about ₹17.5 lakh for the plan, the visit and the fee.

How it works

One verified report, carried lot by lot

Questions

Frequently asked

Doesn't the 97.5% free allocation make the 2026 bill almost zero?

No. The 97.5% factor scales the EU benchmark deduction (1.53 tonnes of CO₂ per tonne of steel), not the exporter's emissions. A bolt filed on India's default carries 6.29 tonnes, so about 4.8 certificates a tonne are still payable. Our calculator shows every step.

Why can't an exporter just verify its own plant?

It should, and Carbon Khata organises that. But most of a bolt's emissions come from the wire rod. Without the mill's report, the rod takes India's default of about 4.3 tonnes plus the mark-up, so the exporter still pays about ₹19,300 a tonne more than it would with the mill's data.

Does Carbon Khata verify the data?

No. Accredited verifiers do. EU impartiality rules bar a verifier from preparing its own client's plan, so we prepare the evidence and the verifier checks and signs it.

Will stockists share their buying prices or customers?

No. The ledger holds tonnes, lot numbers, heat numbers and the mill's emissions report. Prices, margins and customer lists never leave the stockist's books.

Is the GST e-invoice changed?

No. We use the invoice reference number only as a key to match a lot to its report. Emissions data stays in Carbon Khata, so nothing in the GST schema changes.
